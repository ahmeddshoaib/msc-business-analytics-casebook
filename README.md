## Professional thread

The projects cover one connected decision cycle:

- **Describe and integrate:** SQL, data quality, RFM and dashboarding.
- **Predict:** regression, classification, forecasting and text analytics.
- **Optimise:** room scheduling and healthcare capacity allocation.
- **Govern:** leakage checks, validation rules, model comparison and explicit decision boundaries.

That range supports roles in business analytics, supply chain, operations, retail, customer insight, BI and graduate consulting.

## Evidence policy

The public repositories are rebuilt portfolio versions, not edited submissions. I keep exact submissions, feedback and raw university data in a private archive. Public work follows four rules:

1. no student number, marker feedback or private university path;
2. no raw dataset where redistribution rights are unclear;
3. synthetic outputs labelled as synthetic;
4. group work states my own contribution and remains code-private without team consent.

## Coursework not promoted as a portfolio project

The MSc also included an AI-focused writing module. It developed research and communication skills through blog-style assignments, but it does not add stronger evidence than the technical projects above, so it is listed here for completeness rather than published as a separate repository.
